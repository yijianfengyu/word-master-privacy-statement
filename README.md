## 关于 WordMaster 与隐私保护的声明

> **更新日期**：2026年10月4日  
> **生效日期**：2026年10月4日  

---

### 【核心隐私承诺摘要】

> 1. **纯单机与本地化运行**：本应用定位为单机英语单词学习辅助工具，**不获取、不上传、不转让任何用户个人隐私信息**。
> 2. **无服务器上传**：您的背词打卡进度、生词本、测试记录及本地昵称仅保存在您的手机设备本地存储中，我们不设任何远程服务器收集您的个人数据。
> 3. **零第三方追踪**：本应用未集成任何第三方广告 SDK、用户行为分析 SDK 或跨平台追踪组件。

---

“**WordMaster**”（以下简称“本应用”）由开发者团队（以下简称“我们”）为您提供纯粹、专注的高中英语单词学习与自测服务。我们非常重视您的个人信息和隐私保护，严格遵守《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》《中华人民共和国网络安全法》及相关国家标准与监管规范，特制定本《隐私政策与个人信息保护声明》（以下简称“本声明”）。

---

### 1. 我们如何收集和使用您的个人信息

我们坚持**“最小必要”与“用户知情同意”**原则。本应用为完全本地单机离线使用的学习工具，**不存在收集个人身份信息、设备敏感追踪码或生物识别信息的行为**。

#### 1.1 本地业务功能与数据处理说明
- **学习记录与生词本**：当您使用背单词、错词复习、词汇测验等功能时，系统会在您的**本地设备（手机存储空间）**中保存您的学习进度、掌握状态与复习计划。此类数据完全存储于您的设备本地，不会向开发者或任何第三方服务器上传。
- **本地学习者档案（昵称设置）**：若您在应用内创建或切换本地学习者名字（如“小明”、“Emma”），该昵称仅用于本地界面的多用户身份展示与本地数据隔离，无需绑定手机号、身份证或实名认证，亦不会上传云端。

#### 1.2 无个性化推荐与广告推送
本应用**不提供任何基于用户画像的商业广告投放或个性化算法推荐服务**。所有背词词表（如考频顺序、词根词缀、主题分类）均为预置的固定词库算法，不涉及对您个人偏好或行为特征的追踪分析。

---

### 2. 设备权限调用说明

为保障应用的基础功能正常运行，本应用在运行期间遵循最小权限原则：

| 权限名称 | 使用目的 / 场景 | 权限类型 | 是否强制 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **存储权限**<br>`(写入/读取外部存储)` | 仅用于在本地保存/读取您的单词学习进度、生词本配置及导出您的学习记录。 | 基本文件存取 | **否**（支持应用专属私有目录，无需额外授权） |
| **网络访问权限**<br>`(INTERNET)` | 仅用于单词真人发音音频在线流式播放（如有）或检查词库资源更新，不用于收集上传个人信息。 | 基础网络访问 | **否**（支持纯离线发音与纯本地运行） |

> **特别声明**：本应用**绝不调用**通讯录、地理位置（GPS）、相机、麦克风、短信、通话记录、设备敏感标识符等与学习功能无关的权限。您可以随时在手机系统“设置 - 应用权限管理”中关闭上述权限，关闭后不影响基本背单词功能的使用。

---

### 3. 未成年人及青少年隐私保护特别说明

本应用主要面向高中学生及广大英语学习者。我们深知未成年人个人信息保护的重要性：

1. 本应用**不收集任何未成年人的姓名、身份证号、人脸图像、就读学校、联系方式或地理位置**等个人隐私。
2. 本应用不含任何形式的社交聊天、打赏充值、陌生人交友或网络游戏诱导功能。
3. 如果您是未成年人（特别是未满14周岁的儿童），请在监护人指导下阅读本政策并使用本应用。监护人有权随时监督和清理设备上的本地学习记录。

---

### 4. 与第三方共享、转让及公开披露

我们郑重承诺：
- **无共享**：我们不会向任何第三方公司、组织或个人共享您的任何数据。
- **无转让**：我们不会将您的任何数据转让给任何公司、组织和个人。
- **无公开披露**：我们不会公开披露您的任何学习数据或个人信息。

---

### 5. 第三方 SDK 目录与插件说明

为了保障软件的纯粹性与安全性，本应用**未接入任何第三方广告类 SDK、数据分析统计类 SDK 或第三方社交登录 SDK**。

如未来因功能升级（例如接入系统原生语音合成 TTS 引擎等）需引入必要的技术组件，我们将严格进行安全评估并在本页面及时向您公示组件名称、使用目的及处理规则。

---

### 6. 数据安全与保护措施

1. **本地存储安全**：您的所有背词数据与自测记录均存放于操作系统为本应用分配的专属私有数据存储目录中，受操作系统底层应用沙盒（Sandbox）安全隔离机制保护，其他应用无权随意读取。
2. **防泄露与最小化**：由于我们未在远程服务器建立任何用户数据库，因此从根源上杜绝了服务器端数据泄露、黑客拖库或数据滥用的风险。

---

### 7. 您对个人数据的管理权利

您对存储在手机中的本地学习数据享有完全的自主控制权：

- **数据查看与修改**：您可在应用内“复习本 / 单词表”页面直接查看或编辑您的单词状态与笔记。
- **数据重置与完全删除**：您可以通过进入手机系统【设置】→【应用管理】→【WordMaster】→【清除存储数据/缓存】，或直接卸载本应用，即可在您的设备上永久且不可恢复地清除所有本地学习数据。

---

### 8. 隐私政策的更新与修订

为了给您提供更好的服务，本隐私政策可能会随应用版本的更新而适时修订。当条款发生重大变更时，我们将在应用更新版本时通过弹窗、公告或应用内显著位置通知您。若您继续使用我们的服务，即表示同意受经修订的隐私政策约束。

---

### 9. 如何联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问、意见或建议，您可以通过以下方式与我们取得联系，我们将在验证您的身份后 15 个工作日内予以回复和处理：

- **开发者 / 团队名称**：WordMaster 团队
- **联系邮箱**：`support@wordmaster.app` *(请替换为您的实际邮箱)*
- **反馈渠道**：应用内【个人中心 / 设置】→【帮助与反馈】

# 🇺🇸 English Version

## WordMaster Privacy Policy & Personal Information Protection Statement

> **Last Updated**: October 4, 2026  
> **Effective Date**: October 4, 2026  

---

### 【Key Privacy Commitments Summary】

> 1. **Completely Local & Offline Execution**: WordMaster is designed as an offline vocabulary learning tool. **We do NOT collect, upload, share, or sell any of your personal data.**
> 2. **No Remote Server Collection**: Your learning progress, custom vocabulary lists, test history, and local profile nicknames are stored exclusively on your local device. We do not maintain any cloud servers to store your personal information.
> 3. **Zero Third-Party Tracking**: WordMaster does not integrate any third-party ad networks, behavioral analytics SDKs, or cross-platform tracking tools.

---

“**WordMaster**” (hereinafter referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our") is developed to provide a focused, secure, and privacy-first English vocabulary learning experience for students and learners. We take your privacy and personal data protection very seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled in strict compliance with applicable data protection laws and industry security standards.

---

### 1. How We Collect and Process Your Information

We strictly adhere to the **"Data Minimization"** and **"Purpose Limitation"** principles. As a local educational application, **the App does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII), device tracking identifiers, or biometric data.**

#### 1.1 Local Features & Data Processing
- **Learning Progress & Vocabulary Notebooks**: When you study vocabulary, review difficult words, or complete quizzes, the App stores your study history, mastery levels, and schedule **exclusively within your device's local storage sandbox**. This data is never transmitted to us or any third-party servers.
- **Local Learner Profiles (Nicknames)**: If you create or switch local learner names (e.g., "Alex", "Emma"), this information is used solely for local multi-user profile switching on your device. It requires no email, phone number, or account registration, and is never uploaded to the cloud.

#### 1.2 No Personalized Advertising or Profiling
The App **does not perform any algorithmic profiling or behavioral targeting**. All word lists (e.g., frequency order, roots & affixes, thematic categories) are statically pre-built and do not analyze your personal habits for commercial purposes.

---

### 2. Device Permissions Usage

To ensure core functionality while protecting your privacy, the App requests only the minimum necessary permissions:

| Permission Name | Purpose / Scenario | Permission Type | Mandatory |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Storage / File Access**<br>`(READ/WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE)` | Used solely to save/read your vocabulary lists and backup export files locally on your device. | Basic File I/O | **No** (Uses private app sandbox storage by default) |
| **Internet Access**<br>`(INTERNET)` | Used solely for streaming online audio pronunciation (if enabled) or checking vocabulary database updates. | Network Access | **No** (Fully supports offline audio and offline study) |

> **Explicit Statement**: The App **NEVER** accesses sensitive permissions such as Contacts, GPS Location, Camera, Microphone, SMS, Call Logs, or Unique Hardware Tracking IDs. You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time via your device's system settings.

---

### 3. Children & Minors' Privacy Protection

As an educational application designed for high school students and young learners, we are committed to safeguarding minors' online privacy (in compliance with COPPA, GDPR-K, and applicable minor protection regulations):

1. We **do not collect any personal information from children or minors**, including names, physical addresses, facial data, school details, or contact numbers.
2. The App contains no social chat rooms, financial recharge/tipping systems, or gamified payment incentives.
3. If you are under the age of 14 (or the legal age of majority in your jurisdiction), please use this App with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. Parents/guardians have full control to inspect and clear local study data at any time.

---

### 4. Data Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

We solemnly declare that:
- **No Data Sharing**: We do not share your data with any third-party companies, organizations, or individuals.
- **No Data Transfer**: We do not transfer your personal data to any external parties.
- **No Public Disclosure**: We never publicly disclose any of your learning records or personal details.

---

### 5. Third-Party SDKs and Plugins

To maintain a clean and secure software environment, **WordMaster does not incorporate any third-party advertising SDKs, commercial tracking SDKs, or third-party social login SDKs.**

If future versions introduce system-level utility components (such as native text-to-speech engines), we will conduct rigorous security evaluations and update this policy accordingly.

---

### 6. Data Security Measures

1. **Local Sandbox Isolation**: All study records and database files are stored in the application's private sandbox directory, protected by the mobile operating system's underlying security architecture against unauthorized access from other apps.
2. **Zero Server-Side Risk**: Because we do not store your data on external cloud servers, there is zero risk of remote database breaches, server leaks, or identity theft.

---

### 7. Managing Your Data Rights

You retain 100% full ownership and control over your learning data:

- **Access & Edit**: You can view, add, modify, or mark words directly in the "Review / Word List" section within the App.
- **Permanent Deletion**: You can erase all local learning records at any time by going to your phone's **Settings → Apps → WordMaster → Clear Data / Storage**, or by uninstalling the application.

---

### 8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements or regulatory changes. When significant modifications occur, we will notify you through an in-app notice or release notes. Your continued use of WordMaster after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

---

If you have any questions, feedback, or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy compliance team. We will respond within 15 business days:

- **Support Email**: `278950211@qq.com`
